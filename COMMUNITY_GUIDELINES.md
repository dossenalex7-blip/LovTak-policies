# LovTak Community Guidelines

**DRAFT — align these rules with actual moderation tools and procedures before launch.**

LovTak is built for people to Watch, Play, and Connect. Help keep the community safe and respectful.

## 1. Respect people
Do not harass, bully, threaten, stalk, dox, or target people with hateful abuse. Do not encourage harm.

## 2. Protect children
Never groom, exploit, sexualize, solicit, threaten, or endanger a minor. Never create, request, possess, upload, or share child sexual abuse material. Do not ask minors for intimate images, private contact details, or off-platform meetings. Suspected exploitation may be escalated to appropriate authorities as required by law.

## 3. No sexual exploitation
Do not share intimate images without consent, threaten to publish them, or use messages, gifts, or livestreams to pressure or exploit someone.

## 4. No violence or dangerous activity
Do not threaten real-world violence, encourage attacks, coordinate serious wrongdoing, or promote dangerous challenges likely to cause injury.

## 5. No hate or extremist recruitment
Do not dehumanize or attack people based on protected characteristics, recruit for violent extremist activity, or praise violent extremist attacks.

## 6. No scams or manipulation
Do not impersonate others, phish, run fake giveaways, manipulate gifts or payouts, sell fake engagement, or deceive users to obtain money, credentials, or personal information.

## 7. Respect privacy
Do not publish private addresses, phone numbers, financial information, credentials, private messages, or identifying information without lawful authority and appropriate permission.

## 8. Respect intellectual property
Post only content you own or are authorized to use. Do not upload pirated or infringing material.

## 9. Keep livestreams safe
Follow age restrictions and livestream rules. Do not use livestreams to harass, threaten, exploit minors, show dangerous acts, or coordinate illegal activity. Do not evade camera or microphone restrictions.

## 10. Fair play
Do not cheat, exploit bugs, manipulate tournaments, steal accounts, or defraud users in games or virtual-item features.

## 11. Reporting and enforcement
Use in-app reporting where available and provide enough detail for review. Do not submit knowingly false or abusive reports.

Depending on severity, enforcement may include content removal, warnings, feature restrictions, temporary suspension, permanent termination, or referral to authorities where required.
