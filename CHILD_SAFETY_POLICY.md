# LovTak Child Safety Policy

**DRAFT — operational controls and legal reporting procedures must be implemented before publication as final or before relevant features are enabled.**

LovTak is committed to preventing child sexual exploitation and abuse and making the platform safer for children and teens. This draft describes intended commitments; it is not proof that all controls are operational.

## 1. Zero tolerance
LovTak prohibits child sexual abuse material (CSAM), grooming, sexual solicitation, sextortion, trafficking, sexualized depictions or exploitation of minors, adults arranging inappropriate contact with minors, and coercion or requests for sexual images from minors.

## 2. Age eligibility and assurance
The minimum account age and feature-specific age limits must be decided through legal and safety review. A self-declared birthday alone may not be sufficient.

LovTak has considered allowing accounts from age 6. **Do not enable registration for children under 13 in the United States, or any other child age group, until qualified counsel has reviewed applicable laws and required age assurance and verifiable parental consent are implemented.** Other countries may impose different requirements.

Adult livestream camera and microphone features must use reliable eligibility controls. A minor livestream mode, if offered, must be separately risk-assessed and designed to prevent unsafe adult contact.

## 3. Safer defaults for minors
Before minors are permitted, implement age-appropriate defaults, including restricted discoverability where appropriate, limits on unsolicited adult-to-minor contact, location and personal-information protections, controls over comments and messages, restrictions on targeted advertising where prohibited, spending safeguards, reporting and blocking, and age-appropriate recommendations and moderation.

These are required safeguards, not claims that all are currently active.

## 4. Detection and reporting
Provide accessible reporting for suspected child exploitation and urgent safety concerns. Prioritize and review reports through trained personnel and documented procedures.

Where legally required, preserve relevant evidence securely and report apparent CSAM or suspected child sexual exploitation to appropriate authorities or reporting organizations, including NCMEC in the United States where applicable. Personnel must not download, copy, or circulate suspected CSAM outside authorized, legally compliant procedures.

## 5. Enforcement
Accounts and content involved in child exploitation may be immediately restricted or removed. LovTak may preserve information and cooperate with lawful investigations as required. Users must not evade enforcement through replacement accounts.

## 6. Livestream safety
Livestream features must include age eligibility controls, reporting, moderation escalation, and controls to stop harmful broadcasts. Minors must not use adult livestream capabilities.

## 7. Staff access and training
Only authorized personnel with a legitimate safety need may access sensitive child-safety reports or evidence. Access must be limited and logged. Staff handling reports must receive appropriate training and escalation instructions.

## 8. Accountability
Maintain internal records of reports, response times, enforcement actions, and legally required referrals. Public transparency reports should use aggregated information and avoid exposing children or investigations.
